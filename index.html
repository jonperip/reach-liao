About the data

Live arrivals: LTA DataMall via arrivelah. Stops, services and first/last bus: BusRouter SG (scraped from LTA). Maps, place search and journeys: OneMap © Singapore Land Authority.

Times with a small ring are scheduled estimates (no live GPS for that bus). Public holidays follow the Sunday timetable.
